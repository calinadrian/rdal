		<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content"><div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en-GB" dir="ltr"><p><b>Monsters</b> are attackable entities in <i>RuneScape: Dragonwilds</i>. Each monster has a health bar, and a <a href="/w/Power_level" title="Power level">power level</a> depending on the area that the player is in. In addition, there are two types of monsters: campaign and respawnable. Campaign monsters do not respawn, unlike the respawnable variants. For example, certain <a href="/w/Goblin_(race)#Goblin_enemies" title="Goblin (race)">goblins</a> at the centre of <a href="/w/Bramblemead_Village" title="Bramblemead Village">Bramblemead Village</a> are considered campaign monsters and will not respawn after their death.
</p>
