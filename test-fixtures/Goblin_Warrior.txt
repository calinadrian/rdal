<div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Journal">Journal</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/Goblin_Warrior?section=5&amp;veaction=edit" title="Edit section: Journal" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-divider"> | </span><a href="/w/Goblin_Warrior?action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section&#039;s source code: Journal"><span>edit source</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<div class="seealso">The following text is transcribed from the <a href="/w/Journal" title="Journal">Journal</a>.</div><div class="journal-entry">
<p><a href="/w/Goblins" class="mw-redirect" title="Goblins">Goblins</a> were once the minions of the war god, <a href="/w/Bandos" title="Bandos">Bandos</a>, until his death at the hands of <a href="/w/Armadyl" title="Armadyl">Armadyl</a>, the god of justice. Raised on the brutal world of <a href="//runescape.wiki/w/Yu%27biusk" class="extiw" title="rsw:Yu&#39;biusk">Yu'biusk</a>, goblins have known no other existence than one of constant battle and bloodshed. Their religion and society decries education and self-improvement in favour of conflict for conflict’s sake. To run from battle is the greatest sin to goblinkind. 
</p><p>Goblins reproduce quickly and grow up fast. Many of their wars have been won by sheer attrition, as there are always more goblins to hurl at the enemy.   
</p><p>The goblins of <a href="/w/Ashenfall" title="Ashenfall">Ashenfall</a> have turned away from Bandos, putting their faith in the dragon that circles <a href="/w/Ghornfell" title="Ghornfell">Ghornfell</a>. <a href="/w/Velgar" title="Velgar">Velgar</a> is a cruel and uncaring master, yet the goblins live longer lifespans under his watch than they ever did under Bandos. 
</p>
