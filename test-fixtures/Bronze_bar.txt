<p>A <b>bronze bar</b> is a material used to craft weapons and armour. It is created by smelting 3 <a href="/w/Copper_ore" class="mw-redirect" title="Copper ore">copper ore</a> and 3 <a href="/w/Tin_ore" class="mw-redirect" title="Tin ore">tin ore</a> at a <a href="/w/Furnace" title="Furnace">furnace</a>. Receiving the bronze bar in the player's inventory will unlock the following:
</p>
<ul><li><a href="/w/Loom" title="Loom">Loom</a></li>
<li><a href="/w/Sawmill" title="Sawmill">Sawmill</a></li>
<li><a href="/w/Blacksmith%27s_bench" class="mw-redirect" title="Blacksmith&#39;s bench">Blacksmith's bench</a></li>
<li><a href="/w/Armour_bench" class="mw-redirect" title="Armour bench">Armour bench</a></li>
<li><a href="/w/Mystic_forge" class="mw-redirect" title="Mystic forge">Mystic forge</a></li></ul>
